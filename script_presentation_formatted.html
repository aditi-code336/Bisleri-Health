EXECUTIVE BOARD Q&A DEFENSE GUIDE (PRE-EMPTIVE STRATEGY)

❓ Q1. Won’t consumers resist paying ₹40 to ₹60 when regular Bisleri is ₹20?
Defense (Speaker 1 / 3 / 6): "Consumers resist paying ₹40 for water; they eagerly pay ₹60 to ₹150 for functional benefits—as demonstrated by Red Bull (₹125), coconut water (₹60), and electrolyte powders (₹30+). Bisleri Health is not competing with commodity water bottles in grocery crates; it is competing in the quick-commerce wellness basket against RTD nutraceuticals and sports drinks. Furthermore, our premium recyclable packaging (can, carton, glass) creates immediate visual and tactile value differentiation."
❓ Q2. How will our manufacturing supply chain handle cans, paper cartons, and glass bottles alongside standard PET?
Defense (Speaker 5 / 6): "Phase 1 utilizes specialized co-packing agreements with established canning (aluminium) and aseptic carton (Tetra Pak style) facilities in Mumbai and NCR, requiring zero immediate CAPEX on new machinery. Our ₹10 Cr budget funds working capital and tooling. As volumes hit Month 6 milestones (120,000 units/day), we will transition high-speed lines in our existing Pune and Greater Noida plants into dedicated functional bottling lines."
❓ Q3. What prevents local copycats from launching cheap 'electrolyte waters' at ₹25?
Defense (Speaker 1 / 4): "Unorganized players lack three things: FSSAI nutraceutical grade certifications, scientific clinical credibility, and most importantly, Bisleri’s 50-year heritage of trust. In functional health, consumers are terrified of counterfeit or substandard electrolyte formulations. Bisleri’s name guarantees pharmaceutical-grade purity that no local player can replicate."
❓ Q4. How do Smart Vending Kiosks justify their operational maintenance and restocking costs?
Defense (Speaker 6 / 7): "Our Smart Vending Kiosks are equipped with real-time IoT weight and telemetry sensors connected to our local distribution ERP. Route trucks already service nearby grocery and retail outlets daily; restocking a kiosk requires no dedicated logistics run. With an average of 80 cans sold per gym/campus kiosk per day at ₹40, each machine breaks even on operating lease within 4.2 months."
